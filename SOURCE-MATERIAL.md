## What the credit does and does not establish

The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes ideas and methods from their protected expression. Commentary can sometimes qualify as fair use, but that is a fact-specific legal assessment; a free educational site and a small number of excerpts are not automatic safe harbors. The extraction of a lesson into new words does not itself establish that no protected expression was retained.

YouTube permits its embeddable player, while its user-content license does not grant general off-platform reuse rights. Republishing source frames, including in a ZIP, is distinct from linking to or using that player. Applicable national law and platform terms require separate consideration. Publication on GitHub Pages alone does not determine the governing law.

For a clearer permission basis, obtain written authorization covering the particular frames, public display, downloadable distribution and intended agent-reference use. Any third-party material visible in a video may involve other rights holders. Original explanations and timestamped source links can be maintained without offering the frames as downloadable assets. Removal from a current build does not erase older Git commits or copies already downloaded.
