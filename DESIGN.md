# Design direction and review

Brian supplied [Next.js documentation](https://nextjs.org/docs) and [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/) as references, and delegated the UI choice to an Astra reviewer. Both references were inspected as live pages and captured for comparison.

## Selected direction

A warm concept reference library: concise concept discovery inspired by Laws of UX, with the reading and support structure of documentation. The implementation preserves its own cream, charcoal and restrained sage palette. No reference logos, illustrations or proprietary components are copied.

- A direct introduction and one quiet metadata line lead to search and topics.
- Text-led concept cards expose title, mechanism, teaching references and verification paths without decorative glyphs or tall empty space.
- Skill pages keep copy/download actions, a bounded reading measure and section navigation. On narrow screens, a native disclosure exposes the section list without crowding the initial reading view.
- Dated source evidence, original adaptations, setup instructions and limitations remain separate and inspectable.
- Search recovery, clipboard refusal, focus destinations and collection return context are treated as part of the interface.

These decisions apply intent-and-flow, relative-emphasis, typographic-roles, responsive-priority, component-states and evidence-story. A selected direction is not final verification; publication notes record the actual checks and review outcome.
