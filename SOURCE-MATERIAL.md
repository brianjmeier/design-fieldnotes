# Source-frame excerpts and attribution

The six JPEGs in `assets/examples/` are selected still excerpts from three public videos by [Kole Jain](https://www.youtube.com/@KoleJain). The original videos and imagery retain their respective owners’ rights. **These source frames are excluded from the project’s MIT license.** The project grants no additional license to that material and claims no affiliation, endorsement or permission from the creator.

The excerpts accompany original educational commentary about specific design mechanisms. They are not stock assets for incorporation into another product. Citation and provenance are not substitutes for permission. The MIT license covers this project’s implementation and original synthesis; full videos and caption tracks are not distributed.

[examples.json](examples.json) records exact observed player times, image hashes, visible facts, original interpreted mechanisms and comparison limits. Each frame preserves the entire video image at 960 × 540; there is no image retouching. Video-origin annotations and subtitles remain visible where present. Camera framing differs between some source moments; apparent size is not a controlled variable.

For attribution or removal concerns, [open a repository issue](https://github.com/brianjmeier/design-fieldnotes/issues). Future source-image contributions must include the same provenance, distinct licensing scope and contextual commentary. Authored reconstructions must be explicitly labeled and never passed off as video frames.
